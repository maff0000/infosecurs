Backups you can actually restore from

Is important business data backed up, and has anyone tried restoring from it?

Why we ask: a backup nobody has tested is a hope, not a safeguard — ransomware and accidental deletion are the two most common reasons businesses need to restore.

Backups exist, but an untested restore is treated as a gap, not a pass — we'll record this honestly and suggest a test restore as a next action.
